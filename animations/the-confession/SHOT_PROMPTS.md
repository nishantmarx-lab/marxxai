# The Confession — shot prompts (manual workflow)

**How to use**
1. **Keyframe:** in Gemini / ChatGPT, attach the refs listed (`refs/nishant.png`, `refs/maya.png`, `refs/set.png`) and paste the keyframe prompt. Vertical 9:16. Make sure there's no watermark in the frame.
2. **Animate:** in Kling / Hailuo (free tier), upload the keyframe, paste the motion prompt, choose **5s**, 9:16. No audio needed: our voices get added later.
3. Save the clip as `sNN.mp4` (for example `s01.mp4`) and upload it in chat. I trim each clip to its exact length.

Always add this style line to the end of every keyframe prompt:
> 3D animated film still, Pixar-style stylised characters, cinematic, soft global illumination. Deep teal-blue shadows, cold laptop-blue key light, warm amber desk-lamp accent. 35mm lens, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain. Rainy night. Vertical 9:16. Keep characters exactly as in the reference images.

## Shot 1 — 3s — close-up — refs: nishant, set
**Keyframe:** Close-up of Nishant (from reference) sitting at his desk at night, face lit only by the cold blue glow of the open laptop, guilty exhausted expression, eyes glistening, rain-streaked window blurred behind him. Office from the set reference.
**Motion:** Slow push-in on his face. He exhales, then slowly closes the laptop lid; the blue light on his face fades to near darkness. Rain trickles on the window. Mouth closed, no talking.

## Shot 2 — 3s — wide — refs: maya, nishant, set
**Keyframe:** Wide shot of the dark office from the doorway behind Maya (seen from behind, back to camera, tan tote on her shoulder). Nishant sits at the desk far away in near darkness, laptop closed. Rainy window behind him.
**Motion:** Maya steps in and reaches over to switch on the brass desk lamp; warm amber light spills across the desk and Nishant. Camera static. Her face not visible.

## Shot 3 — 5s — medium — refs: nishant, set  (only face-on talking shot)
**Keyframe:** Medium shot of Nishant at the desk under the warm desk lamp, leaning forward, sliding a thick manila folder stamped "CONFIDENTIAL" in red across the desk toward camera. Serious, ashamed expression.
**Motion:** He slowly slides the folder forward across the desk and looks up, speaking quietly and naturally, mouth moving, guilty expression. Subtle camera push-in. Rain behind.

## Shot 4 — 3s — reaction close-up — refs: maya, set
**Keyframe:** Close-up of Maya (from reference) under warm lamp light, looking down at an open manila folder in her hands, eyes widening in shock.
**Motion:** Her eyes widen slowly, brows lift, she stops breathing for a moment. Lips closed. Very slight camera push-in.

## Shot 5 — 4s — insert close-up — refs: set
**Keyframe:** Overhead close-up of an open manila folder on a dark wooden desk under warm lamp light, a woman's hands (charcoal blazer sleeves) holding printed pages of text, each page with a bold tab label: "CLIENT A", "CLIENT B", "CLIENT C". Pages look like typed AI prompts.
**Motion:** Her hands flip quickly through the pages, one after another. Lamp light flickers slightly.

## Shot 6 — 4s — medium two-shot — refs: maya, nishant, set
**Keyframe:** Medium two-shot across the desk under the lamp: Maya, head bowed, pulling her own dark folder out of her tan leather tote; Nishant watching her, confused.
**Motion:** Maya slowly pulls the folder out and starts to open it, head down (face mostly hidden). Nishant's expression shifts from confusion to alarm. No talking.

## Shot 7 — 4s — close-up hands — refs: set
**Keyframe:** Close-up of the desk: a woman's hands (charcoal blazer) holding a half-open dark folder; a man's hand (navy rolled sleeve, black smartwatch) slamming down flat on top of it.
**Motion:** The man's hand slams down and forces the folder shut. Papers jolt. A lightning flash lights the scene from the window. Quick, impactful.

## Shot 8 — 6s (make 5s, I'll extend) — slow push-in two-shot — refs: maya, nishant, set
**Keyframe:** Two-shot profile across the desk: Nishant and Maya locking eyes in tense silence, his hand still on her folder, she still gripping it. Lamp between them, heavy rain on the window behind.
**Motion:** Very slow push-in toward them. Neither moves; intense stare. The desk lamp flickers twice. Rain gets heavier. Mouths closed. End on a held frame.
